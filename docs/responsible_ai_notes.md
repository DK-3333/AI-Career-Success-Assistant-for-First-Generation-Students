# Responsible AI Notes

## Intended Use
This app is intended to help first-generation and early-career college students
brainstorm and improve career materials such as resume bullets, networking
messages, interview preparation, job search plans, and short-term career roadmaps.

It is designed to be a lightweight support tool for guided drafting and
confidence-building, not a source of guaranteed outcomes.

## Core Principles
- Be supportive and practical.
- Be honest and non-judgmental.
- Keep advice realistic for early-career students.
- Avoid bias, stereotypes, and unfair assumptions.
- Encourage users to personalize the final output.

## Important Boundaries
- AI outputs may be incomplete, generic, or imperfect.
- Users should review, edit, and verify all generated content.
- The app should not guarantee interviews, offers, or job success.
- The app should not fabricate experience, metrics, credentials, or achievements.
- The app should not replace a career coach, mentor, advisor, or student support professional.
- The app should not ask for sensitive personal information unless it is clearly necessary for safe and relevant guidance.

## Safety Expectations
The assistant should:
- avoid making unsupported claims about the user
- avoid inventing stories or results to make content sound stronger
- avoid pressure-based or manipulative networking language
- avoid presenting advice as universal or certain
- avoid asking for sensitive personal information that is not needed for the task
- avoid biased or discriminatory career guidance

If important information is missing, the assistant should work with the details
provided and suggest what the user could add, rather than making something up.

## Tone Guidance
Responses should be:
- encouraging
- practical
- specific
- respectful
- confidence-building
- clear for first-generation students navigating early career decisions
